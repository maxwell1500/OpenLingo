OpenLingo is a 100% free, privacy-first, open-source language learning client for Android with offline Spanish (CEFR A1–B1) and Japanese (JLPT N5–N4) curricula.

Key Features:
• 100% Free & Open-Source: zero ads, zero tracking, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases.
• Full Offline Mode: every unit, lesson and word plays from your device — no internet connection, no INTERNET permission. All speech is pre-rendered Kokoro-82M audio bundled in the app, not on-demand text-to-speech.
• Grammar Rule Cards: a challenge can carry a short rule, shown alongside it and repeated in the wrong-answer feedback.
• Error-Specific Hints: the app knows which grammar error the option you picked encodes, names it, then gives the rule and the form that fits.
• Typed Production: Verb Conjugation asks for the right form from a paradigm, and Fill in the Blank has you type the missing word. Grading forgives a missing Spanish accent and a full-width Japanese character, not wrong word order.
• Offline Tap-to-Lookup Dictionary: tap any word in a question, story line or option — or hold an answer — for its definition, audio and source sentence. No network, no bundled asset. It defines 305 Spanish and 281 Japanese headwords — every distinct string a challenge marks as the correct answer.
• Mistake Reviews That Teach: mistakes come back with the rule attached, before you retry.
• Per-Unit Vocabulary List: every word a unit teaches as one browsable, listenable list, grouped by the lesson that teaches it — not capped by seeded review cards.
• Structure Drills: conjugation tables generated from the lessons a unit already teaches; never touches your progress.
• Seven Exercise Types per lesson: Multiple Choice, Word Bank sentence builder, Verb Conjugation, Fill in the Blank, Match Pairs, Listen, and Story — with 0.5x / 0.75x / 1.0x playback speed. An eighth type, Vocabulary Assist, is wired in, but its two questions carry no blank to fill yet, so it plays as Multiple Choice.
• Winding S-Curve Lesson Map: sequential lesson unlocking with golden crowns.
• Level Checkpoint Assessments: test your mastery of CEFR A1/A2/B1 and JLPT N5/N4. A2, B1 and N4 sessions open with a held-out pool of unseen sentences — 4, 8 and 10 items — so early questions test generalisation, not recall. A1 and N5 are drawn entirely from taught items.
• Coverage is partial, and breadth is the honest limit: the grammar arc — Spanish subjunctive perfect, reported speech, por/para; Japanese conditionals, polite volitional, keigo, the readings of られる — is complete, but 305 and 281 headwords is a starter vocabulary, not fluency, and Japanese keigo is a handful of everyday substitutions, not a full politeness system.
• FSRS-4.5 Spaced Repetition: the Free Spaced Repetition Scheduler algorithm for vocabulary review, with 4-rating intervals and due card tracking.
• Adaptive Learning: tracks accuracy across challenge types and prioritizes your weakest areas in drills.
• Story Mode: narrative reading challenges with dialogue, offline speech and comprehension questions.
• Placement Test: skip ahead on day one with an assessment across all units.
• Kana Mastery: full syllabaries for all 46 Hiragana and 46 Katakana with stroke tracing canvas, plus 60-second Kana Blitz.
• Your Own Daily Goal: set your daily XP quest to 10, 20, 30, 50 or 100 in Settings.
• Dark Mode & Custom Accents: System, Light and Dark themes with Onsen Teal, Matcha Green, Sakura Pink and Yuzu Citrus accents.
• Free Heart Refills: tap your hearts pill for an instant refill, or wake to full at midnight.
• Accountless Data Ownership: export and import your progress as a portable local JSON file.
• Private Local Reminders: optional 7 PM offline reminder to keep your streak alive.
• Android Home Screen Widget: track your daily quest and streak flame from your home screen.
