OpenLingo is a 100% free, privacy-first, open-source language learning client for Android with rich offline Spanish (CEFR A1–B1) and Japanese (JLPT N5–N4) curricula.

Guided by our chill Capybara mascot (🦫), learn at your own pace without stress, streaks anxiety, or ads.

Key Features:
• 100% Free & Open-Source: zero ads, zero tracking, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases.
• Full Offline Mode: all units, lessons, and high-quality Kokoro-82M text-to-speech audio play directly from your device without an internet connection.
• Winding S-Curve Lesson Map: sequential lesson unlocking with golden crowns and glowing active nodes.
• Android Home Screen Widget: track your daily quest and streak flame right from your home screen.
• FSRS-4.5 Spaced Repetition: scientific Free Spaced Repetition Scheduler algorithm for vocabulary review with 4-rating review intervals and due card tracking.
• Level Checkpoint Assessments: test your mastery of CEFR A1/B1 and JLPT N5/N4 levels with celebratory badges and auto-queued practice.
• Adaptive Learning: automatically tracks accuracy across challenge types and prioritizes your weakest areas first during practice drills.
• Story Mode: interactive narrative reading challenges with dialogue, offline speech, and comprehension questions.
• Placement Test: skip ahead on day one with an onboarding assessment across all units.
• Rich Exercise Types: Multiple Choice, Assist, Word Bank sentence builder, Match Pairs, Stories, and Listen challenges with a 0.6x slow playback turtle button (🐢).
• Kana Mastery: full syllabaries for all 46 Hiragana and 46 Katakana with stroke tracing canvas.
• Dopamine Polish: celebration confetti showers, 3D golden trophy pop-in, perfect score bonus XP, and combo praise banners.
• Dark Mode & Custom Accents: complete System, Light, and Dark themes with Onsen Teal, Matcha Green, Sakura Pink, and Yuzu Citrus accents.
• Free Heart Refills: tap your hearts pill anytime for an instant free refill, or wake up to full hearts at midnight.
• Accountless Data Ownership: export and import your full progress anytime as a portable local JSON file.
• Private Local Reminders: optional 7 PM offline reminder to keep your streak alive.
