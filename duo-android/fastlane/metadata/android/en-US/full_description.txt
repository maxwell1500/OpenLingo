OpenLingo is a 100% free, privacy-first, open-source language learning client for Android with rich offline Spanish (CEFR A1–B1) and Japanese (JLPT N5–N4) curricula.

Guided by our chill Capybara mascot (🦫), learn at your own pace without stress, streaks anxiety, or ads.

Key Features:
• 100% Free & Open-Source: zero ads, zero tracking, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases.
• Full Offline Mode: every unit, lesson and word plays from your device — including bundled Kokoro-82M text-to-speech audio — with no internet connection and no INTERNET permission at all.
• Grammar Rule Cards: a grammar challenge can carry a short rule shown alongside it and repeated in the wrong-answer feedback, so the reason an answer is right arrives when you get it wrong.
• Error-Specific Hints: a wrong answer diagnoses itself. The app knows which grammar error the option you picked encodes, names it, then gives the rule and the form that fits.
• Typed Production: Verb Conjugation asks for the right form from a paradigm rather than a translation, and Fill in the Blank has you type the missing word. Grading forgives a missing Spanish accent and a full-width Japanese IME character, but not the wrong word order.
• Offline Tap-to-Lookup Dictionary: tap any word in a question, story line or option — or hold an answer — for its definition, its audio, and the sentence it came from. No network, no bundled dictionary asset.
• Mistake Reviews That Teach: your mistakes come back with the rule attached, shown before you re-attempt them, so a repeat mistake is a chance to learn rather than a second guess.
• Per-Unit Vocabulary List: every unit's words as one browsable, listenable list, grouped by topic.
• Structure Drills: conjugation tables generated from the lessons a unit already teaches — a study surface that never touches your progress.
• Seven Exercise Types: Multiple Choice, Word Bank sentence builder, Verb Conjugation, Fill in the Blank, Match Pairs, Listen, and Story — with a playback speed selector (0.5x / 0.75x / 1.0x) on every audio button, to catch the phonemes at your own pace.
• Winding S-Curve Lesson Map: sequential lesson unlocking with golden crowns and a floating START chip on your next lesson.
• Level Checkpoint Assessments: test your mastery of CEFR A1/B1 and JLPT N5/N4 levels with celebratory badges. Questions come from a held-out pool, so passing measures generalisation, not recall.
• FSRS-4.5 Spaced Repetition: scientific Free Spaced Repetition Scheduler algorithm for vocabulary review with 4-rating review intervals and due card tracking.
• Adaptive Learning: automatically tracks accuracy across challenge types and prioritizes your weakest areas first during practice drills.
• Story Mode: interactive narrative reading challenges with dialogue, offline speech, and comprehension questions.
• Placement Test: skip ahead on day one with an onboarding assessment across all units.
• Kana Mastery: full syllabaries for all 46 Hiragana and 46 Katakana with stroke tracing canvas, plus 60-second Kana Blitz.
• Your Own Daily Goal: set your daily XP quest to 10, 20, 30, 50 or 100 in Settings.
• Dopamine Polish: celebration confetti showers, 3D golden trophy pop-in, flawless-lesson bonus XP, and combo praise banners.
• Dark Mode & Custom Accents: complete System, Light, and Dark themes with Onsen Teal, Matcha Green, Sakura Pink, and Yuzu Citrus accents.
• Free Heart Refills: tap your hearts pill for an instant free refill, or wake up to full hearts at midnight.
• Accountless Data Ownership: export and import your full progress anytime as a portable local JSON file you control.
• Private Local Reminders: optional 7 PM offline reminder to keep your streak alive, switchable in Settings.
• Android Home Screen Widget: track your daily quest and streak flame right from your home screen.
